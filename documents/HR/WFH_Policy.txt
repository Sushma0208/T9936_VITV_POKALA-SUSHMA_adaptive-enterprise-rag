Company Work From Home Policy

Policy ID: HR-2026-17
Department: HR
Access Level: Employee, Manager, HR

1. Work From Home Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period are eligible to request work from home.

2. Monthly Work From Home Limit
Employees are permitted to work remotely for a maximum of 8 days per month.

3. Approval Requirement
Prior approval from the reporting manager is required before working remotely.

4. Request Process
Employees must submit a work-from-home request through the company's internal employee portal.

5. Exceptions
Any exception to the monthly limit requires approval from the employee's department head and HR.

6. Policy Version
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 2026
