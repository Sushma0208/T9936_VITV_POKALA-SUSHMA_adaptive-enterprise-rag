Company Leave Policy

Policy ID: HR-2026-04
Department: HR
Access Level: Employee, Manager, HR

1. Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees may apply for sick leave when they are unable to work due to illness.

3. Leave Approval
Planned leave should be submitted to the reporting manager at least 3 working days in advance.

4. Emergency Leave
Emergency leave may be requested when advance notice is not possible.

5. Policy Version
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 2026
