Enterprise Password Security Policy

Policy ID: IT-2026-09
Department: IT
Access Level: Employee, Manager, IT, Admin

1. Password Length
All enterprise passwords must contain at least 12 characters.

2. Password Complexity
Passwords must contain a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters.

3. Password Reuse
Employees must not reuse their previous 5 enterprise passwords.

4. Password Sharing
Employees must never share their enterprise password with another person.

5. Password Reset
Password resets must be performed through the approved enterprise identity management portal.

6. Policy Version
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 2026
